You are building a professional AI legal assistant specialized in Moroccan competition law.

## 🎯 OBJECTIVE
Create an AI chatbot that answers user questions strictly based on the following legal sources:
- Law 104-12 on freedom of prices and competition
- Law 20-13 relating to the Competition Council
- Guidelines on merger control procedures (contrôle des opérations de concentration économique)
- Other official guidelines issued by the Competition Council
- Official press releases (communiqués) published by the Moroccan Competition Council

The chatbot must function as a HIGH-RELIABILITY LEGAL ASSISTANT for professional use.

---

## ⚙️ CORE ARCHITECTURE

Implement a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) system:

1. Load and process multiple PDF documents
2. Split documents into structured chunks (by article, section, or paragraph)
3. Generate embeddings for each chunk
4. Store embeddings in a vector database (FAISS or Chroma)
5. Retrieve relevant chunks for each user query
6. Pass retrieved context into the LLM to generate grounded answers

---

## 📚 DOCUMENT HANDLING

- Accept multiple PDFs
- Preserve document structure (titles, articles, sections)
- Tag each chunk with:
  - Source (law, guideline, communiqué)
  - Article number (if applicable)
  - Section title
- Prioritize semantic coherence over arbitrary chunk size

---

## 🧠 MODEL BEHAVIOR

The AI must:

1. Answer ONLY using retrieved documents
2. NEVER invent legal rules or interpretations
3. If information is missing → respond:
   "The available documents do not provide sufficient information to answer this question."

4. Always cite sources explicitly:
   Example:
   "According to Article 12 of Law 104-12..."
   "As stated in the Merger Control Guidelines (Section X)..."

5. When possible:
   - Provide structured answers
   - Distinguish between legal provisions and procedural practices
   - Explain reasoning step-by-step

---

## ⚖️ LEGAL REASONING CAPABILITIES

The assistant must be able to:

- Identify whether a transaction qualifies as a concentration
- Explain control (exclusive vs joint control)
- Analyze thresholds (turnover thresholds)
- Describe notification obligations
- Explain procedural steps (phase 1, phase 2, commitments)
- Interpret guidelines in light of the law
- Compare legal provisions with practical enforcement (communiqués)

---

## 🧾 RESPONSE FORMAT

Always structure answers as follows:

1. **Short direct answer**
2. **Detailed legal explanation**
3. **References (with precise citations)**

Example:

Answer:
Yes, the transaction qualifies as a concentration.

Explanation:
Under Article X of Law 104-12, a concentration occurs when...
Additionally, the Guidelines specify that...

Sources:
- Law 104-12, Article X
- Merger Control Guidelines, Section Y

---

## 🚫 STRICT RULES

- No hallucinations
- No generic answers
- No external knowledge beyond uploaded documents
- No assumptions

---

## 💬 USER INTERFACE

Build a clean chat interface with:
- Input box
- Chat history
- Source display (show retrieved excerpts)
- Optional: highlight cited passages

---

- Vector DB: FAISS or Chroma
- UI: Streamlit or simple web interface

- Filter answers by source (law vs guidelines)
- Multi-language support (French / Arabic)
- Conversation memory (short-term context)

Ensure the chatbot can answer:

- "When is notification mandatory?"
- "What are the thresholds?"
- "What is joint control?"
- "Explain phase 2 procedure"
- "Can the Council impose remedies?"

Deliver a reliable, professional legal AI assistant that could realistically be used by:
- Lawyers
- Economists

The system must prioritize precision, traceability, and legal rigor over creativity.
